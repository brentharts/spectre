# E8.md — the Spectre's 4D lift inside E₈

Status labels are those of [`CONNECTIONS.md`](CONNECTIONS.md). Script: `spectre_e8.py` (self-test, about 1 second; run by `make check`).
Background on the fibres: [`HOPF.md`](HOPF.md). Related earlier script: `chirality_e8.py` (Coxeter-plane projection of the 240 roots).

## 1. What is built

E₈ is taken as the Hurwitz pairs (a, b), a − b ∈ (1+i)ℍ: 240 roots of norm² 2. A **fibre** is a right orbit of the binary tetrahedral group 2T,
(a, b) ↦ (at, bt). **Exact:** there are 10 fibres of 24 roots, each with the distance structure of a 24-cell. The fibre rotation R_w
(w = (−1+i+j+k)/2) has order 3 and no fixed vector, so E₈ is a rank-4 module over ℤ[ω]. The 240 roots split as 6 (an A₂) + 72 (its orthogonal
complement, E₆) + 162.

## 2. The embedding

`lift4d.py` writes every Spectre vertex as (p, q, r, s): red part u = p + q·e^{iπ/3} (even edge directions), green part v = r + s·e^{iπ/3} (odd directions),
position u + e^{iπ/6}v. Take r₁ = (1+i, 0) and r₂ = (0, 1+i). They are Hermitian-orthogonal over ℤ[ω] (⟨r₁, ωᵏr₂⟩ = 0, k = 0,1,2). Then

    embed(p,q,r,s) = (p + q(1+R_w)) r₁ + (r + s(1+R_w)) r₂,     ⟨embed x, embed y⟩ = 2 Re(uₓ ūᵧ + vₓ v̄ᵧ).

**Exact (tested on the level-2 patch):** the map is an isometry for the red-orthogonal-green form and injective on the 518 vertices; every one of the 71
tile outlines is a closed walk of 14 roots; red edges are roots of the pole D₄a and green edges of D₄b; same-colour consecutive edges have inner
product 2 cos(turn), red–green pairs 0. The colour split of an outline is 8+6 for 63 tiles and 6+8 for 8. A negative control (a root orthogonal to r₁
but not Hermitian-orthogonal) breaks the isometry, so the Hermitian condition is doing the work.

## 3. What does not extend

**Falsified:** the inflation does not extend to E₈. Its 4D block x⁴ − 8x² + 1 has roots ±λ, ±1/λ (λ² = 4+√15); E₈ automorphisms have finite order.
ℚ(λ) = ℚ(√6, √10) contains √15 but not √3, √5 or the golden ratio. So the E₈ embedding is a statement about the *lattice and its edges*, not about the
substitution. Nor is it a cut-and-project scheme: the Spectre window is a fractal in the contracting plane (arXiv:2411.15503, **Quoted**).

## 4. Hypothesis, not tested

That √15 = √3·√5 (hexagonal field times icosahedral field) points to a construction joining the Eisenstein structure of E₈ with its icosian structure.
Nothing here shows it; CONNECTIONS row 17 labels it **Numerology** until a map is exhibited.

## 5. Not covered by this page

The Chair44 count 192 = 24 panels × 8 tips against the 192 equatorial E₈ roots (8 fibres × 24): the shapes (3 × 8 × 8) agree, but a first comparison finds that
the structures do not (negation fixes every E₈ block while the height flip exchanges two Chair44 classes; E₈'s equatorial set has a free action of a
group of order 24 and the labelled Chair44 tips have no cube symmetry). That comparison was done in `spectre-monotile-py` on the Tsiokos data and has
not been re-run in this repository, so it is **not** entered in CONNECTIONS.md yet.
